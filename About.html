About Me

I am Sonia A.J., a multi-talented professional based in Sacramento, California, where I live with my husband and four children. A dedicated lifelong learner, I hold B.A Degrees in Business Management, Public Administration and Developmental management, alongside associate degrees in Human Resources and Social Science. Beyond my academic achievements, I am certified nurse committed to serving my community’s health needs over ten years.

With over 20 years of experience, my career spans the banking, aviation, and healthcare industries. My versatile skill set is backed by certifications in records management, airline operations, and more than 10 great achievements rewards. From the fast-paced corridors of aviation and banking to high touch world of healthcare, I have built a career on the principle of service and blends analytical precision with deep human empathy.

A true “multipotentialities, as a mother of four, I understand the art of balance and the nurturing growth-traits that extend into my vibrant personal life as an avid cook, Gardener, and Painter. I am a gifted culinary artist known for my tremendous ability and capability to make different varieties of foods, breads, and pastries for birthdays and weddings events. Some of my favorite bread types known by neighborhoods are French Breads, Garlic Breads, Milk Breads, seeded whole wheat/grain Breads, Raisins Breads, Easy rolls and Chocolate Breads.

Whether I am gardening, painting or baking my signature French and garlic breads, my passion for creativity shines through. My love for the culinary arts has evolved into professional event catering venture, and looking ahead, I aim to launch my own restaurant and online platform while continuing my advanced studies in health sciences.
